### A Quick Reality Check on Trailer Theories

Trailer analysis is fun, but it has a failure mode: treating a two-minute edit as a plot summary. A few habits keep speculation honest:

1. Separate what you *saw* from what you *inferred*.
2. Check whether a claim traces back to Marvel, Disney, or a credentialed trade outlet.
3. Expect the final series to include material the trailer never touched.
4. Revisit the trailer after the premiere — the rewatch is usually more revealing than the first pass.
