### Why a Mentor Fits Vision's Arc

Vision has always been the MCU's most philosophical hero. He was born fully grown, questioned his own humanity within days, and has died, been rebuilt, and lost everything twice. A character with that résumé doesn't need another origin story — he needs a purpose.

Mentoring a younger team gives him one. It also mirrors the comics, where Vision's role as a steadying influence among teenagers is one of his most underrated arcs.

If you want to read the Vision Quest vision new suit the way dedicated lore hunters do, here's a practical checklist. These habits will help you separate real signals from wishful thinking.

Three quick tips for doing this well:

- **Compare against the comics, not other MCU suits.** Vision's design language comes from specific comic eras, so the source material is the better yardstick.
- **Wait for two independent confirmations.** A single set photo or leaked image is easy to misread, especially for a character whose costume has subtle color shifts.
- **Separate "confirmed" from "reported" from "rumored."** Community reports are useful, but they aren't official statements, and treating them as fact leads to disappointment.

Following those rules keeps your expectations grounded while still letting you enjoy the detective work that makes following a long-running franchise fun.
