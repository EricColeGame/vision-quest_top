## What VisionQuest Is About

Vision was introduced to movie audiences in *Avengers: Age of Ultron*, where J.A.R.V.I.S. was placed into a Vibranium body originally intended for Ultron, animated by the Mind Stone. After his death in *Avengers: Infinity War*, the character was rebuilt in *WandaVision* — and that is where this new series picks up.

In VisionQuest, Vision is rebooted and living in hiding after escaping people who wanted to use him as a weapon. Searching for purpose, he consults the AI personas embedded in his programming — F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., J.A.R.V.I.S., and Ultron. A bounty on his head forces him on the run alongside Thomas Shepherd, a young man who may be connected to Billy Maximoff. The central mystery is Thomas's identity, and the central threat is Ultron's influence.
